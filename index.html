Why this

One secure vault for every guest touchpoint

Like Marriott’s operating systems power brand licensing more than real estate, 120Vault™ is the IP that lets ZenoVerse license wellness protocols across 120BodySpa™, 420WellnessJamaica™, and studios worldwide. It sits in experiential, non-invasive biohacking and mindset transformation for high-profile guests (CEOs, senior litigators, family-office principals) — while staying strictly non-clinical.

Strategic partner, not a vendor

Fractional CTO arm for Steph and Pascal: relieve operational load, ship an investor-grade asset for institutional diligence, and hand over cleanly. Pedigree across Ola, Zendrive, and Google AlloyDB.

Non-clinical by design

Lifestyle measures and vetted protocols (coMra, InnergizeX) — no diagnose/treat features in these phases. Avoids diagnostic liability while delivering institutional-grade tracking.

Built for Jamaica realities

Offline-first staff tools for the mountain estate, mobile-first guest flows, JDPA / GDPR / CCPA-aware consent and audit.

Phase 1 scope

Eight modules. One foundation.

Everything below ships in the $38,500 Phase 1 build. Household / Chief of Staff is an MVP; full 120ClubVIP™ Passport expands in Phase 3.

M1 Identity & MFA M2 Guest profile & VIP prefs M3 Consent & encrypted vault M4 Engagement log M5 Offline staff PWA M6 RBAC & delegations MVP M7 Audit & compliance M8 GoHighLevel bridge Later: measures dashboard Later: Club Passport / mobile Later: multi-brand & wearables

What each module delivers

  • M1 Identity — Staff MFA (TOTP/SMS); guest magic link + Argon2id; session auto-lock on shared devices
  • M2 Profile — Digitized intake; dietary / cultural / privacy flags; emergency & household metadata
  • M3 Vault — Purpose-based waiver vs media consent; AES-256-GCM envelope encryption + KMS; e-sign audit
  • M4 Log — coMra, InnergizeX, coaching, excursions; duration + observations + concierge flags
  • M5 Offline PWA — iPad staff console; IndexedDB cache; optimistic sync with conflict rules
  • M6 RBAC — Practitioners see assigned guests only; concierge blocked from wellness notes; CoS MVP toggles
  • M7 Compliance — JDPA / GDPR / CCPA·CPRA / WA MHMDA; append-only audit; export & erasure rights
  • M8 GHL — Exchanges with GoHighLevel; does not replace it; payment tokens only — never raw cards

Explicitly out of Phase 1

  • Wellness Week 3-point measures & Mind/Body/Soul dashboard → Phase 2
  • Post-retreat coaching, 120ClubVIP™ Passport, member mobile, Mandarin → Phase 3
  • Multi-property tenancy, wearables/IoT, executive analytics → Phase 4
  • Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace calendar sync
  • Greenvelope RSVP
  • Future clinical program module (separate brief later)

GHL keeps funnels, Curated Plan Assessment calls, and payment processing. 120Vault™ returns onboarding status tags without raw wellness data.
